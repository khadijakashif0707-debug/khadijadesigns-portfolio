Featured Case Study Buttr— melt the mood
Bakery & Coffee Full Identity Packaging Merch Social

Buttr is a modern bakery café built on warmth and simple indulgence — the smell of fresh croissants, the first coffee of the day. The brief was to make that feeling visible.

I built the identity around a soft, melting mascot, a chunky rounded wordmark, and a warm terracotta-and-cream palette — then carried it across signage, packaging, merch, print and social so every touchpoint feels like the same warm hand made it.
